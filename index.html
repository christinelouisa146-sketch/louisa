What is Malware?

Understanding malicious software

💻 Meaning of Malware

Malware is short for malicious software. It refers to programs or code designed to damage, disrupt, spy on, or gain unauthorized access to computers, networks, and mobile devices without the user's consent.

Types of Malware

Explore the four common types of malware below.

🦠

Virus

Malware that attaches itself to files and replicates.

🐛

Worm

Malware that automatically spreads across networks.

🐴

Trojan

Malware disguised as legitimate software.

🔒

Ransomware

Malware that encrypts files and demands payment.

🦠 Computer Virus

What is a Virus?

A computer virus is malware that attaches itself to legitimate files or programs and replicates by inserting its code into other programs.

How does it spread?

Through deceptive emails, suspicious links, infected downloads, and unsecured networks.

What damage can it cause?
  • Steal sensitive personal data.
  • Delete or damage important files.
  • Reduce computer performance.
  • Spread infection to other systems.
How to prevent it
  • Enable automatic software updates.
  • Avoid suspicious links and downloads.
  • Use reliable antivirus software.

🐛 Computer Worm

What is a Worm?

A computer worm is standalone malware that automatically replicates and spreads across computer networks without attaching itself to an existing program.

How does it spread?

By exploiting software vulnerabilities, malicious email attachments, and infected USB drives.

What damage can it cause?
  • Consumes network bandwidth.
  • Slows down internet speeds.
  • Steals or deletes files.
  • Creates security backdoors.
How to prevent it
  • Keep operating systems updated.
  • Avoid unexpected email attachments.
  • Use a firewall and antivirus software.

🐴 Trojan Horse

What is a Trojan?

A Trojan horse is malware disguised as legitimate or useful software. It tricks users into downloading and installing it.

How does it spread?

Through fake applications, games, software downloads, and suspicious email attachments.

What damage can it cause?
  • Creates hidden backdoors.
  • Steals passwords and banking information.
  • Records keystrokes.
  • Downloads additional malware.
How to prevent it
  • Download applications from trusted sources.
  • Do not open suspicious documents.
  • Scan new downloads with antivirus software.

🔒 Ransomware

What is Ransomware?

Ransomware is malware that locks a victim's device or encrypts their files, holding the data hostage until a payment is made.

How does it spread?

Through phishing emails, software vulnerabilities, compromised accounts, and other malware infections.

What damage can it cause?
  • Encrypts important files.
  • Steals sensitive information.
  • Disrupts business operations.
  • Can prevent access to important services.
How to prevent it
  • Enable multi-factor authentication.
  • Maintain regular backups.
  • Install software updates.

LEARN FROM HISTORY

Major Cyberattacks Timeline

Explore significant cyberattacks and understand their impact.

2000 — ILOVEYOU

The Love Letter Worm

The ILOVEYOU worm spread through email with a message that appeared to contain a love letter. When recipients opened its attachment, the worm spread to other contacts and affected millions of computers worldwide.

Threat: Email worm

2010 — Stuxnet

The Stuxnet Attack

Stuxnet was a sophisticated computer worm that targeted industrial control systems. It demonstrated how cyberattacks could affect physical infrastructure.

Threat: Computer worm / industrial cyberattack

2017 — WannaCry

The Global Ransomware Attack

WannaCry ransomware spread rapidly across vulnerable Windows computers. It encrypted files and demanded ransom payments, disrupting organizations in many countries.

Threat: Ransomware

2024 — Change Healthcare

Healthcare Cyberattack

A ransomware attack against Change Healthcare disrupted healthcare payment and prescription services in the United States. The incident highlighted the importance of protecting sensitive medical information and securing access to systems.

Threat: Ransomware / data theft

How to Stay Protected

Six important cybersecurity habits.

🔄 Update Software

Enable automatic updates and patch security vulnerabilities.

🔐 Strong Passwords

Use strong, unique passwords and multi-factor authentication.

📩 Avoid Suspicious Links

Never open unexpected links or email attachments.

🛡️ Antivirus

Use reliable antivirus and firewall protection.

💾 Backup Files

Keep regular offline backups of important data.

📱 Trusted Downloads

Only download applications from official and trusted sources.
